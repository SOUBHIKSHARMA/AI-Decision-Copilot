# AI Decision Copilot 📊🤖
### AI-Powered Business Intelligence & Analytics Workspace

AI Decision Copilot is an interactive business intelligence application that transforms raw CSV datasets into meaningful business insights, statistical summaries, visualizations, and actionable recommendations.

The goal is to help business users understand their data, identify trends, investigate performance, and make more informed decisions without manually analysing every record.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Business Intelligence Dashboard
- Overview of analysed datasets and total records.
- Automated KPI calculations.
- Revenue, cost, profit and profit margin analysis.
- Dataset completeness indicators.

### 2. CSV Data Explorer
- Upload structured CSV datasets.
- Explore records through an interactive data table.
- Filter rows using search.
- Inspect individual columns and their properties.

### 3. Data Quality & Statistical Profiling
- Missing-value detection.
- Duplicate-record identification.
- Automatic column type detection.
- Mean, median, minimum, maximum and standard deviation.
- Pearson correlation analysis.
- Dataset completeness reporting.

### 4. Interactive Visualizations
- Custom chart builder.
- Bar, line, area and pie charts.
- Group data by different categorical or date columns.
- Compare business metrics using different aggregation methods.
- Automated dataset visualizations.

### 5. AI-Powered Business Insights
- Generate executive summaries.
- Identify important business trends.
- Highlight unusual patterns and anomalies.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.
- Display supporting evidence and analytical limitations.

### 6. Ask Your Data
- Ask natural-language questions about uploaded datasets.
- Receive answers grounded in calculated dataset statistics.
- Explore regional, product and category-level performance.

### 7. Executive Reports
- Generate consolidated analytical reports.
- View KPIs, statistical profiles and visualizations.
- Include AI-generated findings and recommendations.
- Export reports in CSV and JSON formats.
- Print or save reports as PDF through the browser.

## 🎯 Product Objective

AI Decision Copilot was developed around a simple problem:

Business users often have access to large amounts of structured data but struggle to convert it into understandable insights and decisions.

The application brings data exploration, statistical analysis, visualization and AI-assisted interpretation into one workspace.

## 🔮 Future Improvements

- Support for Excel and additional file formats.
- Advanced filtering and segmentation.
- More customizable dashboards.

- Larger dataset support and performance optimization.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Analysis depends on the quality and structure of uploaded datasets.
- AI-generated recommendations should be reviewed before business decisions are made.
- Correlation does not establish causation.
- Results from small sample datasets may not generalize to larger populations.
- Local browser storage is not a substitute for secure cloud-based persistence.
